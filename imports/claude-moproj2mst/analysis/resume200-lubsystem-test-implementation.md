The four-record in-memory assertion is gated by loaded evidence for exactly three slots and objects, position-to-engine IDs 1–3, defined kinds, and exactly one master candidate at position 1 under the source-qualified kind/slot rule. No derived BCC transition expectation is asserted for targeted objects.
