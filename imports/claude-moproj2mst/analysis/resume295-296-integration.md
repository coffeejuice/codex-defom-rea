CP296's immutable-original PE audit recovered exact per-component packing order: scalar, B, G, R each undergoes binary32 multiplication by 255, signed 64-bit truncation and low-byte selection, yielding a raw `[R,G,B,scalar]` word. The two scalar values are established as 0.99f and +0.0f, giving top bytes `0xfc` and `0x00` in the ordinary finite domain. The zero-flag route passes palette slot 12 as a float triple; the nonzero route calls imported QColor `redF`, `greenF`, `blueF` in that order and narrows each double before both packs. Preparation occurs even with count zero.
