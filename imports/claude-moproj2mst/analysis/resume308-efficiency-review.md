Their final combined `unittest` runs had 41, 69 and 78 cases and reported approximately 0.017, 0.035 and 0.031 seconds of test execution. The shell calls observed by the orchestrator were under one second for each non-logging run. These measurements exclude review, repair, fixture/clone setup and Git transport; no measured end-to-end or token speedup follows.
