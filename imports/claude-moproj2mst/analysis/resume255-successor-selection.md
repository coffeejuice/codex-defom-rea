PDIE has a valid independently PASS-reviewed bounded plan, but its current integration tests depend on optional historical fixtures and accept Skip. In addition, the migration's native timing claim must stay narrower: current C++20 assignment evaluates vt120 RHS before the second simCtl LHS, while retained d0.c resolves the second native pointer before the virtual call. That domain qualification remains required even after tests pass.

Thus SPRING can use already-present unconditional caller timing coverage plus direct finite output tests without first establishing whether optional corpus cases executed or constructing a new hook-level coverage path. PDIE remains ready after that shared-file reservation, not blocked or rejected. No measured time/token advantage is claimed.
